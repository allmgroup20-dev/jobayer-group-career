# Part 06 — Growth Operating System (AIOS)

> Canonical AIOS Part 06. The audit layer's `24_GROWTH_OS.md` implements this part.

## 6.1 Growth Pillars

1. **Viral engine** — measurable, natural invite loops (referral, share-to-unlock, leaderboards, WhatsApp share).
2. **Organic domination** — SEO, free Bengali content, WhatsApp distribution, no paid ads.
3. **Experimentation engine** — a living 50-experiment backlog, prioritized by ROI + single-founder effort.
4. **Growth scorecard** — KPIs tracked continuously (Part 10 dashboard).

## 6.2 Viral Loop Requirements

- A clear **trigger** (benefit after purchase/unlock) → **share action** (WhatsApp/referral) → **reward** (৳ or unlock) → **new user acquisition** → loop.
- Measure the **K-factor**; target K ≥ 1 for self-sustaining growth.
- Anti-fraud: loop must be protected from self-referral/farming abuse (Part 08).

## 6.3 The 50-Experiment Engine

- The audit layer maintains **50 concrete, ordered, evidence-backed experiments** in `24_GROWTH_OS.md` (P0-priority first), each with: hypothesis, mechanics, effort (single-founder), expected impact, metric, and anti-abuse note.
- Experiments feed Part 11's 20-experiments-per-30-day cadence.
- No experiment may rely on dark patterns or fake social proof (Part 01 §1.4.6).

## 6.4 Growth Scorecard

- Domains: Acquisition (referral/WA), Activation, Retention, Revenue/LTV, Virality (K-factor), Trust.
- Each **/100**, weighted, tied to evidence; refreshed on the KPI dashboard cadence (Part 10).
