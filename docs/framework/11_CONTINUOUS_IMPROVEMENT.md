# Part 11 — Continuous Improvement Engine (AIOS)

> Canonical AIOS Part 11. The audit layer's `42_CONTINUOUS_IMPROVEMENT.md` implements this part.

## 11.1 Cadence

| Cycle | Mandate | Owner |
|---|---|---|
| Every 30 days | **20 experiments** run/refined (from the 50-experiment backlog, Part 06) | Founder + AIOS |
| Every 90 days | **20 product improvements** shipped (from findings + KPIs) | Founder + AIOS |

## 11.2 Experimentation Rules

- Every experiment: hypothesis, mechanics, metric, sample, duration, success threshold, anti-abuse note.
- Results recorded honestly — a failed experiment is a valid result.
- No dark patterns (Part 01 §1.4.6).

## 11.3 Improvement Loop

1. Review KPI deltas (Part 10 dashboard).
2. Select improvements from audit findings (P2/P3) + user feedback.
3. Ship → measure → update scorecard & registers → traceability matrix refresh.

## 11.4 KPI Monitoring

- Continuous monitoring via Part 10 KPI framework; anomalies open a new experiment or a new finding.
